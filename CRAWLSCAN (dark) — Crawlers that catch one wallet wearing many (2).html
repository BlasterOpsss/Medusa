how it works

06
01 · CRAWL

Read the holders

Pulls the top holders straight from the chain. Pools, burn and router addresses are filtered out, so only real wallets are left.

02 · TRACE

Walk each history

Each crawler checks how old a wallet is, who funded it, how many transactions it made, and whether it hit the first blocks after launch.

03 · LINK

Tie wallets together

Connects wallets run by one person: tokens moved between them, a shared funder, or fresh wallets that all bought in the same block.

04 · VERDICT

Count the people

Linked wallets collapse into operators. Five signals roll into a 0–100 score with a one-line reason: CLEAN, OK, RISKY or DANGER.
